DomlyCurrency is a currency-symbol subset of Noto Sans Regular.
Source: Debian fonts-noto-core, /usr/share/fonts/truetype/noto/NotoSans-Regular.ttf.
Only Unicode currency symbols U+20A0–U+20CF and space are retained.
The subset is bundled to render ₸ offline when the primary font lacks it.
