## Implemented

- Payment retry displays the remaining invoice amount, bonuses already applied, and additional bonuses selected now. The existing spending limits are unchanged.
- Order cancellation refunds outstanding bonus debits even if the original Kaspi invoice was cancelled and a later payment attempt exists. Customer/order locks and the ledger prevent repeated or concurrent refunds. Previous partial refunds are deducted. Legacy orders without debit ledger rows retain a field-based fallback; production recovery still requires reconciliation.
- Admin “Тексты и переводы” loads every dictionary page, maps server fields correctly, includes bundled phrases before synchronization, and edits Russian and Kazakh text under stable keys. Source synchronization preserves edits. Parameterized Russian and Kazakh messages retain their live values.
- The legal/information editor reads and writes the same canonical document. Full text and Kazakh text are preserved; summaries have separate JSON metadata. `content_pages.presentation` is an additive migration. Legal pages load independently of the general catalog, retain scroll state, show a scrollbar, and expose loading failures instead of silently presenting a substitute document.
- Residential areas returned by OSM are expanded into actual addressed buildings within their closed polygon. The lookup is bounded and cached. Almatau/Astana returned four addresses: Kabanbay Batyr 29, 29/2, 29/4, and Turan 32/2. Nearby buildings outside the polygon are excluded. This implementation expands OSM way boundaries; it does not establish completeness for every residential complex or relation boundary.
